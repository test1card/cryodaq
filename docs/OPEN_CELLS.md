# OPEN cells — Montana partial-checkpoint register

**Scope at `8a383a5a`.** This register is the PR's disclosure of what remains
unsealed; it is not a claim that every C1/C2/C3 surface is sealed, that physical
OFF has been proven, or that the candidate is approved.  A row marked
**unexamined** records missing evidence, not a claim that the defect is present.
In particular, this head cannot claim Kimi's no-known-live-instance bar: the
static dashboard still routes a `Т*` channel as temperature, and the periodic
report renderer still orders `Т*` channels specially.

`Fail-closed?` answers the operator-relevant question. **No** means the path can
continue or present a determined-looking result without the stated fact. **Unknown**
means the surface was not examined, so neither refusal nor safe continuation is
established. “Yes, but” identifies a safe rejection that still leaves the product
or evidence claim incomplete. Empty scan roots fail in every guard checked; that
good property does not make an unmatched bypass fail closed.
